## 🔍 Research Domains & Question Framework

### Domain 1: Career Advice for Tough Situations
**Goal:** Build evidence-based playbooks for gaps, layoffs, pivots, and other challenging scenarios.

#### 🎯 Core Research Questions
1. **Gap Narratives:** What psychologically validated frameworks exist for framing career gaps (caregiving, health, sabbaticals) to signal intentionality rather than red flags?
2. **Layoff Communication:** How do executive coaches structure "layoff talk tracks" to depersonalize the event and pivot to skill investment?
3. **Career Pivot Bridges:** What methodologies map transferable skills between dissimilar industries (e.g., Education → Tech, Non-profit → Corporate)?
4. **Over/Under-Qualification:** What language reassures hiring managers about senior candidates not demanding executive pay or leaving quickly?
5. **Ageism Mitigation:** What's the statistical optimal resume history length (10 vs. 15 vs. 20 years)? How to balance contemporary fluency with senior depth?

#### 🔎 Search Queries
- `"career gap framing" psychological research 2020-2026`
- `"layoff explanation" executive coaching best practices`
- `"career pivot transferable skills" O*NET taxonomy`
- `"overqualified candidate" hiring manager psychology study`
- `"resume length ageism" 2024 2025 2026`
- `"ATS age discrimination" resume parsing algorithms`

#### 📚 Key Sources to Target
- **Academic:** Journal of Vocational Behavior, Academy of Management Journal
- **Industry:** Harvard Business Review, SHRM, LinkedIn Talent Solutions reports
- **Data:** Bureau of Labor Statistics, O*NET, Lightcast
- **Studies:** The Ladders resume eye-tracking studies, Workday ATS parsing research

#### 📊 Specific Datasets & Studies
| Study | Focus | Why It Matters |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| The Ladders 7.4-Second Study | Recruiter resume scanning patterns | Optimize resume top-third |

### Domain 4: Forensic Stylometrics & Voice Mimicry
**Goal:** Analyze writing voice mathematically for authentic mimicry.

#### 🎯 Core Research Questions
1. **Stylometric Metrics:** What mathematical measures best capture writing voice?
   - Sentence length distribution & variance (burstiness)
   - Lexical richness (Type-Token Ratio, Yule's K)
   - Syntactic cadence (active vs. passive, compound sentences)
   - Punctuation signatures (em-dashes, semicolons, Oxford commas)
   - Rhetorical stance (assertive vs. collaborative language)
2. **Sample Extraction:** What 3 writing samples best reveal unfiltered voice?
3. **Few-Shot Conditioning:** How to feed stylometric constraints into LLMs for authentic output?
4. **Voice Consistency:** How to ensure cover letters, emails, and resumes sound like the same person?

#### 🔎 Search Queries
- `"stylometry" authorship attribution machine learning 2020-2026`
- `"writing voice analysis" NLP Python library`
- `"burstiness" sentence length variance writing style`
- `"Yule's K" lexical richness calculation`
- `"Type-Token Ratio" text analysis`
- `"rhetorical stance detection" NLP`
- `"few-shot prompting" voice mimicry LLM`
- `"writing style transfer" neural networks`

#### 📚 Key Sources to Target
- **Academic:** Journal of Forensic Linguistics, Digital Scholarship in the Humanities
- **Libraries:** Python's `stylometry`, `textstat`, `spaCy`, `NLTK`
- **Research:** Authors attribution studies, style transfer papers
- **Industry:** Grammarly style analysis, Hemingway Editor algorithms

#### 📊 Stylometric Fingerprint Metrics
| Metric | Calculation | What It Reveals |
|--------|-------------|------------------|
| Avg Sentence Length | Total words / sentence count | Concise vs. elaborate |
| Sentence Length Variance | Standard deviation of lengths | Burstiness/rhythm |
| Type-Token Ratio | Unique words / total words | Vocabulary richness |
| Yule's K | 10,000 * (sum(ni²) - N) / N² | Lexical diversity |
| Active/Passive Ratio | Active verbs / passive verbs | Directness |
| Punctuation Frequency | Count per 1000 words | Writing rhythm |
| Readability Score | Flesch-Kincaid, Gunning Fog | Complexity level |
| Rhetorical Questions | Count per paragraph | Engagement style |

## 🚀 Research Methodology

### Phase 1: Broad Discovery (Week 1)
- **Goal:** Map each domain with 3-5 high-quality sources
- **Approach:** Use web search with broad queries, identify key studies and frameworks
- **Output:** Annotated bibliography for each domain

### Phase 2: Deep Dive (Week 2-3)
- **Goal:** Extract actionable insights and data from primary sources
- **Approach:**
  - Read full papers/studies (not just abstracts)
  - Cross-reference findings across sources
  - Identify contradictions and gaps
- **Output:** Domain-specific research briefs

### Phase 3: Synthesis (Week 4)
- **Goal:** Integrate findings into implementable feature specifications
- **Approach:**
  - Map research insights to specific features
  - Develop algorithms based on proven methods
  - Create validation criteria
- **Output:** Feature specification documents with research backing

### Phase 4: Validation (Ongoing)
- **Goal:** Test assumptions with real user data
- **Approach:**
  - A/B test different advice strategies
  - Measure feature effectiveness
  - Iterate based on feedback
- **Output:** Performance metrics and iteration plans

## 📂 Source Organization System

### Credibility Scoring
Each source will be rated on a 5-point scale:
- **5/5:** Peer-reviewed academic paper, government data, primary research
- **4/5:** Industry report from reputable org, well-cited whitepaper
- **3/5:** Blog post from recognized expert, conference talk
- **2/5:** Opinion piece, anecdotal evidence
- **1/5:** Unverified claim, social media post

### Source Tracking Template
```markdown
- **Source:** [Title](URL)
- **Credibility:** X/5
- **Last Updated:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Key Findings:** [Bullet points]
- **Relevance:** [How it applies to our features]
- **Limitations:** [Potential biases, sample size, etc.]
```
