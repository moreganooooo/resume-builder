WHAT STAYS INSTANT — AND WHY
Screen changes. Menu → screen and back is --dur-instant. A full-screen transition in a terminal is a full repaint; springing it reads as lag, not polish.
Selection. The ┃ bar jumps. It is the cursor, and a cursor that eases arrives after the keystroke that moved it.
Text. Nothing containing words fades in. A glyph mid-fade is an unreadable glyph.
Springs run on geometry and colour only — widths, offsets, bar fills, badge scale. Harmonica integrates per frame at 60fps, so a spring is driven by a tick message, never a duration: the numbers above are the CSS equivalents for this kit. RESUME_BUILDER_MOTION=reduced and prefers-reduced-motion snap every one of them to target.
