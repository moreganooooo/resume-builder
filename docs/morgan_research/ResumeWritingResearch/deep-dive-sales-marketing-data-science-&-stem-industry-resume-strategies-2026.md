## 📊 Executive Summary: 12 Ranked Takeaways

### 💼 **Sales Industry**
1. **Metrics are non-negotiable**: Every bullet must include at least one number—quota attainment, revenue, deal size, or growth percentage
2. **CRM keywords dominate**: Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, Gong, LinkedIn Sales Navigator appear in 97%+ of sales leadership job postings
3. **Summary formula**: Role + years + vertical + quota attainment + deal metrics + primary tools (e.g., "Senior AE with 8+ years in SaaS, 142% quota attainment, $6.2M ARR, Salesforce/Outreach")
4. **Promotion calls**: Explicitly state promotions with quota data (e.g., "Promoted from SDR to AE in 14 months based on 160% quota attainment")

### 📢 **Marketing Industry**
5. **Keyword density**: Use **10-20 hard skill keywords** directly matching the job description, emphasizing SEO, PPC, CRM tools
6. **Top 2026 keywords**: GA4, Revenue Operations, AI Copywriting, Go-to-Market Strategy, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
7. **Channel organization**: Structure bullets under specific channels (paid search, paid social, SEO/content, lifecycle/email, analytics)
8. **Always spell out**: Write "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)" once, then use "SEO" after

### 🔬 **Data Science Industry**
9. **Portfolio > Degrees**: 3-4 complete, deployed projects (RAG systems, AI agents, LLM apps) beat academic credentials for most roles
10. **GitHub strategy**: Include **plain-text URLs** (not hyperlinks) to 2-3 clean, well-documented repos; link to live demos if possible
11. **ATS target**: Aim for **~70% keyword match** with job description, clean single-column formatting
12. **Project formula**: What you built + the tech + quantified outcome (e.g., "Built production RAG system with 95% accuracy, deployed on AWS")

### 🧪 **STEM/Science Industry**
13. **Lab skills focus**: Hard lab skills (operating instruments, safety protocols, sample handling, running experiments) must be specific and contextual
14. **Publication strategy**: Include publications with **impact factors**, grant funding, research techniques, mentoring experience
15. **Format choice**: **Chronological** works best for most lab roles; CV for academic positions, 2-page resume for industry
16. **AI in labs**: Name specific **laboratory information systems** and **AI-assisted data analysis tools**—this is a growing differentiator

### 1.2 Content & Formatting Rules

**The Golden Rule:** *Every sales bullet must include at least one number.*

**Bullet Point Framework (CAR Method):**
```
[Context] + [Action] + [Result with Metric]
```

**Examples:**
- "Led 14-person sales team to **118% quota attainment**, driving **$6.2M ARR growth** through MEDDIC methodology and weekly Salesforce pipeline reviews"
- "Built and presented monthly sales forecasts and revenue analytics dashboards in **Tableau** to VP of Sales, achieving **95% forecast accuracy**"
- "Maintained a **3.2% cold email reply rate** through A/B testing of subject lines and personalization tactics, versus a **1.5% team average**"
- "Booked **74 qualified meetings** in FY2025 at **118% of annual quota**, with a **3.2% cold email reply rate** (2.1x industry average)"

**Summary Formula (Copy-Paste Ready):**
```
[Role] + [years] + [vertical/industry] + [quota attainment] + [deal metrics] + [primary tools]
```

**Filled Example:**
> "Senior Account Executive with 8+ years in enterprise SaaS sales, consistently achieving 142% of annual quota and securing President's Club honors for three consecutive years. Proficient in Outreach, Salesforce, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, and ZoomInfo."

### 2.3 ATS Considerations

**Critical Rules:**
1. **Always include both** full name and abbreviation on first use:
   - "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)" → then "SEO"
   - "Pay-Per-Click (PPC)" → then "PPC"
   - "Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)" → then "ROAS"
   - "Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)" → then "CAC"

2. **Match 70%+ of job description keywords** for optimal ATS scoring

3. **Digital marketing generalists** should prioritize: Google Analytics 4, HubSpot, SEO, PPC, A/B testing (appear in 80%+ of job descriptions)

4. **Specialization matters**: An SEO-focused JD and a paid media JD pull from **almost entirely different keyword sets**

**ATS Screening Process:**
1. **Keyword pass** (ATS filters for matching terms)
2. **Recruiter scan** (quick human review)
3. **VP/Director read** (closer examination)

Each layer wants the same evidence: **GA4 and HubSpot fluency, channels owned, and CAC/ROAS metrics**

### 3.3 Portfolio & GitHub Strategy

**GitHub Best Practices:**
1. **Plain-text URLs only** (not hyperlinks)—ATS systems parse plain text better
2. **2-3 clean, well-documented repos** (not 20 half-finished projects)
3. **Include README files** with clear project descriptions
4. **Link to live demos** if possible
5. **Test your links**: Open resume in plain-text viewer or ATS parser simulator

**Kaggle Profile:**
- Include **only if Expert rank or above**
- Mention specific competitions and rankings

**Portfolio Platforms:**
- **GitHub Pages** (for project demos)
- **Streamlit** (for interactive apps)

- **Personal website** (for comprehensive portfolio)

### 5.3 Universal Best Practices (All Industries)

**1. Quantify Everything:**
- Sales: Revenue, quota %, deal size, growth rate
- Marketing: Traffic, conversion rate, ROAS, CAC, budget size
- Data Science: Accuracy %, performance metrics, data volume, speed improvement
- STEM: Test volume, accuracy rate, time saved, efficiency gained

**2. Match Job Description Language:**
- Use **exact terms** from the job posting
- Mirror **phrase structure** and **keyword frequency**
- Prioritize **hard skills** over soft skills (70-80% weight in ATS)

**3. ATS Optimization:**
- **Single-column format**
- **Standard fonts** (Arial, Calibri, Times)
- **10-12pt size**
- **No tables, graphics, or complex formatting**
- **DOCX format** for best parsing
- **Plain-text links** (not hyperlinks)

**4. Structure:**
- **Contact Information** (with portfolio links)
- **Professional Summary** (industry-specific formula)
- **Work Experience** (reverse chronological)
