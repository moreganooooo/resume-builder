## 📊 Executive Summary: 10 Key Rules from the Best Platforms

### 1. **The 5 Essential Sections (Non-Negotiable)**
Contact Information → Professional Summary → Work Experience → Skills → Education
*Optional: Certifications, Projects, Languages, Volunteer Work*

### 2. **STAR Method is Universal**
All top platforms (Enhancv, Teal, Rezi, Kickresume, ResumeWorded) use **STAR** (Situation-Task-Action-Result) as their primary bullet point framework, though **compressed** for resumes (Action + Result prominent, Situation/Task often implicit)

### 3. **Reverse-Chronological Order**
Work Experience and Education **must** be listed newest-first - this is an ATS requirement, not just a preference

### 4. **Quantifiable Achievements > Responsibilities**
Platforms **penalize** duty-focused bullets and **reward** metric-driven achievements (e.g., "Increased sales by 30%" vs "Responsible for sales")

### 5. **Hard Skills First, Categorized**
Skills sections should **prioritize hard/technical skills** over soft skills, and be **grouped by category** (Technical, Tools, Certifications, Languages) for ATS parsing

### 6. **Job Description Matching is Mandatory**
All platforms **extract keywords** from job postings and **score resumes** on alignment. Top performers (Teal, Jobscan) provide **real-time match percentages**

### 7. **Single-Column, Clean Formatting**
**No tables, no multi-column, no graphics** - these break ATS parsers. DOCX format often parses **better than PDF** for ATS systems

### 8. **Summary Formula**
**[Years of Experience] + [Profession/Field] + [Top 2-3 Skills] + [1-2 Key Achievements]** = 2-4 sentences max

### 9. **Personalization Depth**
- **Applicant:** Platforms analyze your work history, skills, achievements, and career goals
- **Company:** Platforms extract job requirements, keywords, company values, and industry standards
- **Output:** Content is **dynamically generated** to bridge both profiles

### 10. **What Gets Interviews**
**Customized, achievement-focused, keyword-optimized** content with **specific metrics** outperforms generic resumes by **115%** (Huntr data: 5.75% vs 2.68% conversion)

## ✨ Part 3: Bullet Point Frameworks - The Content Engine

### 3.1 STAR Method: The Universal Standard

**STAR = Situation, Task, Action, Result**

All top platforms use STAR as their **primary bullet point framework**, though with **resume-specific adaptations**.

#### **Full STAR Breakdown**

| Component | Definition | Example | Resume Adaptation |
|-----------|------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Situation** | Context/challenge | "Company had 3-day support backlog" | Often **implicit** in resume |
| **Task** | Your responsibility | "Reduce response time to <24 hours" | Often **implicit** in resume |
| **Action** | What you did | "Rebuilt ticket triage rules" | **Explicit** in resume |
| **Result** | Quantifiable outcome | "Cut response time to 8 hours" | **Explicit** in resume |

**Source:** [ResumeWorded - STAR Method Guide](https://resumeworded.com/star-method-resume-key-advice) (5/5, July 2026)

#### **Resume STAR Compression**

**Interview (Full STAR):**
> "Our support queue had grown to a three-day backlog (Situation), and I was asked to get response times back under 24 hours (Task). I rebuilt the ticket triage rules and wrote canned responses for the 20 most common issues (Action), which brought average response time down to 8 hours within a month (Result)."

**Resume (Compressed STAR):**
> "Cut average support response time from 3 days to 8 hours in one month by rebuilding ticket triage rules and templating the 20 most common responses."

**Key:** On resumes, **Action + Result** carry the line; **Situation + Task** provide context that can be **inferred**

**Source:** [ResumeWorded](https://resumeworded.com/star-method-resume-key-advice) (5/5, July 2026)

### 3.2 STAR Template (Copy-Paste Formula)

**Basic Formula:**
```
[Action Verb] + [What You Did] + [Quantified Result]
```

**Example:**
```
Achieved [result, with a number] by [what you did], [context - only if the line needs it].
```

**Filled Example:**
```
Achieved a 30% increase in revenue by performing valuation analysis on 150+ accounts, securing an average of 10 new customers a year.
```

**Source:** [ResumeWorded](https://resumeworded.com/star-method-resume-key-advice) (5/5, July 2026)

### 3.3 Alternative Frameworks

#### **CAR Method (Context, Action, Result)**
- **Similar to STAR** but combines Situation + Task into **Context**
- **Used by:** Some platforms as a **simplified alternative**
- **Example:** "Facing a 3-day support backlog (Context), I rebuilt triage rules (Action), cutting response time to 8 hours (Result)"

**Source:** [Rezi - STAR Method for a Resume](https://www.rezi.ai/posts/star-method-for-a-resume) (5/5)

#### **XYZ Formula**
- **Structure:** "Accomplished X by doing Y, resulting in Z"
- **Example:** "Increased customer retention by 25% by implementing a new onboarding program, resulting in $50K annual revenue growth"
- **Used by:** Teal, ResumeGenius

**Source:** [Teal - STAR Method Guide](https://www.tealhq.com/post/star-method) (5/5, April 2026)

### 3.5 What Makes a Bullet Point "Excellent"?

**✅ DO:**
- **Start with strong action verb** (Spearheaded, Optimized, Orchestrated, Drove)
- **Include quantifiable metric** (numbers, percentages, dollar amounts)
- **Focus on achievement** (what you accomplished, not what you were responsible for)
- **Use keywords from job description** (exact phrasing for ATS)
- **Keep it concise** (1-2 lines, 6-8 words ideal)
- **Use active voice** ("Managed team" vs "Team was managed by me")

**❌ DON'T:**
- Start with "Responsible for" or "Duties include"
- Use **passive voice**
- Write **paragraphs** (use bullet points)
- Include **irrelevant details**
- **Keyword stuff** (unnatural repetition)
- **Fabricate metrics** (be truthful)

**Effectiveness Data:**
- **Achievement-focused bullets** get **115% more interviews** than duty-focused (Huntr: 5.75% vs 2.68%)
- **Metric-inclusive bullets** are **3.5x more likely** to get interviews (Huntr analysis of 1M+ applications)

**Source:** [Jobstrack.io - AI Job Application Tools](https://jobstrack.io/blog/ai-job-application-tools) (4/5, 2026)

## 🎯 Part 4: Skills Section - The ATS Optimization Layer

### 4.1 Skills Categorization (Best Practice)

**Recommended Structure:**
```
SKILLS

Technical Skills: Python, SQL, JavaScript, React, Node.js
Tools & Platforms: AWS, Docker, Git, Jira, Salesforce
Certifications: PMP, AWS Certified, Google Analytics
Languages: English (Fluent), Spanish (Professional)
```

**Why:**
- **ATS parsers** extract skills more accurately from **categorized lists**
- **Recruiters** can scan quickly for **relevant skills**
- **Avoids** the "chaotic list" problem (JobShinobi)

**Source:** [JobShinobi - Resume Sections](https://www.jobshinobi.com/blog/best-resume-sections-to-include-in-2026) (5/5, 2026)

### 4.2 Hard Skills vs Soft Skills

| Skill Type | Definition | ATS Value | Recruiter Value | Platform Treatment |
|------------|------------|-----------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Hard Skills** | Technical, teachable abilities | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | **Prioritized** by all platforms |
| **Soft Skills** | Behavioral, interpersonal traits | ⭐⭐ | ⭐⭐⭐ | **Limited** (2-3 max) |

**Hard Skills Examples:**
- Programming languages (Python, Java, C++)
- Software/tools (Photoshop, Excel, Salesforce)
- Methodologies (Agile, Scrum, Lean)
- Certifications (PMP, CFA, AWS Certified)
- Industry-specific knowledge (GAAP, HIPAA, SEO)

**Soft Skills Examples:**
- Communication, Teamwork, Problem-solving
- **Warning:** These are **overused** and **devalued** by recruiters

**Platform Approach:**
- **Enhancv:** Generates **separate hard/soft skills lists** from job description
- **Teal:** **Prioritizes hard skills** in matching algorithm
- **Rezi:** **Focuses on hard skills** for ATS optimization
- **Jobscan:** **Flags missing hard skills** from job description

**Source:** [Novoresume - Skills Section](https://novoresume.com/career-blog/resume-sections) (5/5, February 2026)

### 4.4 Skills Formatting Rules

**✅ DO:**
- **Categorize** skills (Technical, Tools, Certifications, Languages)
- **Prioritize** hard skills over soft skills
- **List 8-15** most relevant skills
- **Use exact phrasing** from job description
- **Include proficiency levels** (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert)

**❌ DON'T:**
- **Chaotic list** (no categories)
- **Soft skills only** (recruiters ignore these)
- **Irrelevant skills** (don't list "Microsoft Word" for a developer role)
- **Overstuff** (20+ skills looks desperate)
- **Generic terms** ("Hard worker" - meaningless)

**Pro Tip:** **Mirror the job description's language** - if they say "project management," don't say "PM" or "managing projects"

**Source:** [Jobscan - Top Resume Keywords](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/top-resume-keywords-boost-resume/) (5/5)

## 🎓 Part 5: Education & Certifications - The Credentials Layer

### 5.1 Education Formatting

**Standard Format:**
```
EDUCATION

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
University of California, Berkeley
Graduated: May 2020 | GPA: 3.8/4.0 (Optional)
Honors: Summa Cum Laude, Dean's List (6 semesters)
Relevant Coursework: Data Structures, Algorithms, Machine Learning
```

**Key Rules:**
1. **Reverse-chronological order** (newest first)
2. **Degree Name** (full, not abbreviated)
3. **University Name** (full, not abbreviated)
4. **Graduation Date** (Month, Year - **required** for ATS)
5. **GPA** (Only if **3.5+**, recent graduates only)
6. **Honors** (If notable)
7. **Relevant Coursework** (Entry-level candidates only)

**Source:** [Jobscan - Resume Sections](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/resume-sections/) (5/5)

### 5.2 When to Include/Exclude GPA

| Scenario | Include GPA? | Rationale |
|----------|--------------|-----------|
| **Recent Graduate (0-2 years)** | ✅ Yes (if 3.5+) | Demonstrates academic achievement |
| **Experienced Professional (3+ years)** | ❌ No | Work experience > GPA |
| **GPA < 3.5** | ❌ No | Doesn't add value |
| **Non-US Education** | ✅ Yes (if strong) | International context |

**Google's Stance:**
> "GPAs are **worthless as criteria for hiring**" - Senior VP of People Operations at Google

**Source:** [Novoresume - Education Section](https://novoresume.com/career-blog/resume-sections) (5/5, February 2026)
